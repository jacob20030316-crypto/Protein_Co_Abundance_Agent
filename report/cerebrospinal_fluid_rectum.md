### Function

This is a structural extracellular-matrix and basement-membrane module. The primary evidence is the strong and coherent enrichment for ECM-receptor interaction (KEGG ECM-receptor interaction - Homo sapiens (human); q_global = 4.84e-06), focal adhesion (KEGG Focal adhesion - Homo sapiens (human); q_global = 1.9e-05), integrin signaling (KEGG Integrin signaling - Homo sapiens (human); q_global = 7.44e-05), extracellular matrix and external encapsulating structure (GO:CC external encapsulating structure; q_global = 0.000112)(GO:CC extracellular matrix; q_global = 0.000112), extracellular-matrix structural constituent activity (GO:MF extracellular matrix structural constituent; q_global = 0.000284), basement membrane (GO:CC basement membrane; q_global = 0.000845), laminin trimer and laminin-network terms (GO:CC laminin trimer; q_global = 0.0272)(GO:CC solid phase of basement membrane; q_global = 0.0272)(GO:CC laminin network; q_global = 0.0272), and basement-membrane organization (GO:BP basement membrane organization; q_global = 0.0272). The collagen, laminin, perlecan, and fibrillin components indicate an assembled matrix that can provide tissue support, cell adhesion, and signaling interfaces. PI3K-Akt enrichment (KEGG PI3K-Akt signaling pathway - Homo sapiens (human); q_global = 1.9e-05) is consistent with matrix-to-cell signaling, but it should be interpreted as pathway context rather than proof of pathway activation. The muscle-structure-development term (GO:BP muscle structure development; q_global = 0.00623) may reflect matrix associated with stromal or smooth-muscle environments. The infection and cancer KEGG terms (KEGG Human papillomavirus infection - Homo sapiens (human); q_global = 1.9e-05)(KEGG Toxoplasmosis - Homo sapiens (human); q_global = 0.00243)(KEGG Small cell lung cancer - Homo sapiens (human); q_global = 0.00996)(KEGG Amoebiasis - Homo sapiens (human); q_global = 0.0127) are most plausibly pathway-overlap annotations driven by shared ECM and adhesion genes, not evidence that the module represents infection or cancer.

**Cell composition.** The module is overwhelmingly composed of secreted extracellular-matrix proteins: the HPA summary annotates every module gene as secreted, and the individual entries identify secretion to the extracellular matrix for the collagen, laminin, and perlecan genes. HPA indicates that most genes are not detected in immune cells, with only GSN showing immune-cell enhancement in eosinophils. The strongest single-cell enrichments are fibroblasts and fibro-adipogenic progenitors, with additional representation of hepatic stellate, smooth-muscle, vascular, epithelial, and related stromal populations. Tissue-enhanced signals include placenta, cervix, ovary, smooth muscle, blood vessel, fallopian tube, adipose tissue, and heart muscle. Thus, this shared cerebrospinal-fluid and rectum module should not be interpreted as a broadly expressed housekeeping program or as a blood-cell module. It may instead reflect extracellular matrix produced by shared stromal or vascular-associated cell types, extracellular-matrix material present in the sampled fluids, or a common matrix-remodeling state. The cerebrospinal-fluid HPA protein field has no informative matching tissue result, whereas rectum has mixed detection across the module, so the shared fluid-tissue interpretation remains observational rather than proof of identical cellular origin.

### Novelty

The biological theme itself is not a new molecular discovery: the module contains canonical collagen, laminin, perlecan, and fibrillin components with extensive STRING support, hu.MAP complex support, disease associations, and substantial literature. The potentially new aspect is the reproducible co-abundance of this particular ECM configuration across cerebrospinal fluid and rectum. However, the supplied evidence does not compare this module with published cross-tissue co-abundance modules, so it cannot establish that the cross-tissue pattern is novel. Absence of hu.MAP entries for some genes and absence of STRING partners for GSN do not demonstrate novelty; they may reflect database coverage, interaction type, or context. The defensible conclusion is that this is a biologically credible, partly well-known ECM module with a potentially interesting cross-tissue distribution that requires independent replication and comparison with existing network studies.

**Hypothesis.** Hypothesis: a shared stromal or vascular-associated extracellular-matrix program contributes to the co-abundance pattern in cerebrospinal fluid and rectum. In this model, collagen-I and collagen-VI provide fibrillar and microfibrillar support, laminin assemblies organize basement membrane interfaces, perlecan links matrix components with cell-surface and signaling molecules, and fibrillin contributes force-bearing microfibrils. Integrin engagement with this matrix could couple adhesion to PI3K-Akt and related focal-adhesion signaling, while gelsolin may reflect a blood-associated or actin-remodeling component superimposed on the matrix signal. This is a mechanistic hypothesis, not a demonstration of active signaling, secretion by the same cells, or identical matrix architecture in the two tissues.
